Layer 2 · Start today

Keep a decisions file. Let AI answer from it.

1 · After any meeting

Add every decision from these notes/transcript to my decisions file, one line each: the date, what we decided, why, and who decided.

If one replaces an older decision, mark the older one as replaced.

2 · After a few weeks

Answer this question using my decisions file.

  • If I've already decided it, give the answer and show me where I said it.
  • If the question is missing something, tell me what to ask the person who sent it.
  • If it's a new call, give me your recommendation and leave it for me.
